<h1>🪣 Bucket</h1>

Keep track of what you love. > Bucket is a beautifully designed, social tracking app that lets you curate and share your favorite movies, books, music, and places with your friends.

<h2>✨ Features</h2>

- Categorized Lists: Easily manage your favorite items across Movies, Books, Music, and Places.

- Social Feed: See what your friends are adding, rating, and reviewing in real-time.

- Premium UI/UX: A tactile, immersive interface featuring custom glassmorphism, interactive 3D folders, and WebGL iridescent backgrounds.

- External Integrations: Search seamlessly for media via integrated APIs (TMDB, Google Books, iTunes, etc.).

- Rate & Review: Drop your thoughts and a 5-star rating on any item you save.

<h2>🛠 Tech Stack</h2>

<h3>Frontend:</h3>

- React (Vite)

- Tailwind CSS (Custom styling & Glassmorphism)

- Lucide React (Icons)

- OGL / Three.js (WebGL Shaders & Backgrounds)

- React Router

<h3>Backend & Database:</h3>

- Node.js & Express

- Supabase (PostgreSQL Database, Authentication, RLS)
